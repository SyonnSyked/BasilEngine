# BasilEditor Branding

The confirmed direction is a mature, restrained cyberpunk identity built on a
dark graphite/blue-black field with electric cyan as the primary accent and
restrained violet as the secondary accent.

The application icon will use a simple geometric basil leaf constructed from
angular circuit-like segments. It must remain recognizable at small window and
taskbar sizes and must avoid excessive detail or decorative noise.

- `source/` holds editable master artwork and design notes.
- `icons/` holds reviewed PNG, ICO, and future platform exports derived from
  the master artwork.

The selected BasilEditor mark is the geometric circuit leaf in
`source/basil-editor-icon.svg`. Platform exports must be derived from that
master without independently changing its geometry or palette.
